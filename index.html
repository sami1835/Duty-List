About this Duty Roster

Department of CSE, Varendra University. My Duties shows personal assignments. Full Roster filters by date, exam, room and teacher. Script Receive has a separate duty PDF. Swap Duty searches the currently published roster for valid two-way exchanges. Contact the operator to apply an official change, then check the updated roster and Duty Updates.

Calendar exports include a 20-minute reminder. Script Receive duties have no supplied time and are exported as full-day events.
